If you have booked a pickup from Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK), check the meeting point and assigned vehicle before you arrive. TRider pickups meet at **Gate 7**, with detailed meeting instructions available after booking.

Still arranging your transfer? [Open airport pickup booking in English](/booking?service=AIRPORT_PICKUP&lang=en), choose **Suvarnabhumi (BKK)** and enter your destination address to check the final price before confirming.

## What to check before arrival

Once your vehicle is assigned, you can see its photo in advance. Keep it with your booking confirmation and airport meeting instructions so you can identify the correct car when you meet the driver.

Choose a vehicle that fits both your group and luggage.

Enter your passenger and luggage details to receive a suitable vehicle recommendation.

When travelling with parents or extra luggage, check the suitcase count as well as the number of passengers.

## Meeting point at Suvarnabhumi Airport

Meet your driver at **Gate 7**. Follow the instructions provided after booking rather than relying on an assumed route inside the airport.

These instructions are for a reserved TRider pickup at BKK. Public taxi queues and bus boarding points have their own procedures. If your ticket says **Don Mueang (DMK)**, use your DMK booking instructions; BKK's Gate 7 directions do not apply.

## What if my flight is delayed?

If your flight is delayed, pickup is adjusted to your actual arrival time. Enter the correct flight details when booking. If your plans change, use the contact details in your reservation instructions to reach the team.

## What is included in the pickup price?

For a BKK-to-Pattaya transfer, the route's base fare includes highway tolls and airport parking. There is no late-night surcharge. Flight-delay waiting is included, and pickup is adjusted to your actual arrival time.

The **final booking amount** depends on your actual destination address and any optional services you select. Review any address-based distance charge and selected extras before confirming. A name board is a paid option, not an automatically included service.

For vehicle base fares and route comparisons, see the [Suvarnabhumi-to-Pattaya transfer guide](/blog/suvarnabhumi-to-pattaya-en). Use the booking form with your actual address and luggage details to check your own final amount.

## Requesting a name board

A name board is available as a paid optional service. If you need one, check the request option when booking.

## Pickup checklist

- Read the meeting instructions after booking
- Confirm Gate 7 as the meeting point
- Check the assigned vehicle photo
- Make sure the vehicle fits your group and suitcases
- Request a name board separately if needed

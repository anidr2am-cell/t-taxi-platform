When comparing airport transport in Thailand, a low headline fare does not tell you everything about the journey. A useful comparison starts with the same airport, exact destination, vehicle and luggage requirements, then checks what is included.

This checklist helps you review a private airport transfer quote for Bangkok or Pattaya before you book.

## Compare the same route and direction

Check whether the quote starts at Suvarnabhumi (BKK), Don Mueang (DMK) or your accommodation. An airport arrival pickup and a hotel-to-airport journey are different bookings. Bangkok city and Pattaya are also different destinations.

Give the exact hotel address. A broad destination name may hide differences in the distance or pickup location. If your route includes another stop, tell the provider before accepting the quote.

## Ask whether the price is per person or per vehicle

T-Rider's airport transfer fares are quoted per vehicle. When comparing with transport sold per passenger, calculate the cost for your whole party rather than comparing one ticket with a private vehicle total.

Choose a vehicle that fits your group and luggage before dividing the cost among travelers. A lower vehicle price is not useful if the passengers and bags do not fit together.

## Check each inclusion and possible extra

Before confirming a quote, check:

- Highway tolls and airport parking.
- Any late-night surcharge.
- Flight-delay waiting and the handling of itinerary changes.
- Exact pickup and drop-off addresses.
- Extra stops or separate waiting requests.
- Optional name signs or other requested services.
- Vehicle suitability for the passengers and luggage.

T-Rider includes highway tolls, airport parking and flight-delay waiting in airport pickup fares and states that there is no late-night surcharge. However, an address-based distance charge or optional name-sign fee may still apply. Review the final quote for your own route instead of treating a base fare as a universal total.

For a public metered taxi, bus or other service, consult that provider's current fare and fee information. T-Rider's inclusion policy should not be assumed to apply to another transport service.

## Check luggage before choosing the cheapest vehicle

Count suitcases and bulky items as well as passengers. Golf bags, strollers and oversized luggage may change the vehicle you need. Enter the full combination in the booking form and ask the team about anything unusual.

If you need a child seat, check availability and any applicable charge before confirming. Do not infer equipment availability from the vehicle category alone.

## Understand waiting, payment and cancellation

For airport arrivals, T-Rider states that pickup timing adjusts to actual arrival when a flight is delayed. Keep the team informed if the flight number or itinerary changes. Extra stops, unrelated waiting and a changed hotel pickup are separate questions to confirm.

No deposit is required. Cash can be paid to the driver at the destination; card payments and bank transfers are arranged through the team. Confirm the payment method you want to use before the journey.

For a trip that has not started, cancellation through booking lookup is free when more than two hours remain before pickup. For later changes or cancellation, contact the team for the applicable arrangements. Keep a copy of the booking confirmation and agreed total.

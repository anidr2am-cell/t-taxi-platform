Comparing golf offers for your trip? Start with the terms of the **Pattaya golf price match guarantee**. TRounding covers every product it sells. Rather than simply claiming a low price, the guarantee compares published prices for equivalent products on the same terms, then refunds the difference or matches the price.

## What does the Pattaya golf price match guarantee cover?

The **Pattaya golf price match guarantee** applies to all products sold by TRounding. Another provider's published offer can be compared when it is equivalent to the TRounding product and has the same terms. If a lower published rate is confirmed, TRounding refunds the difference or matches that amount.

There are no exclusions. You do need material showing both the product details and the published price so the terms can be compared. The assessment is based on the actual offer and its conditions, rather than the other provider's name.

## What to send when you apply

To request the **Pattaya golf price match guarantee**, send the administrator material showing another provider's published price.

- A quotation showing the product terms and price
- A promotional poster showing the price and what is included
- A screenshot of a public webpage
- Other publicly available material that confirms equivalent terms

Send the material through [TRounding Naver Band](https://band.us/@trounding) chat. Once it has been checked, the administrator will explain the price match or difference refund. For the golf bar's location and visiting information, ask through Naver Band chat too.

## Check that the offers are equivalent

Similar-looking prices do not always mean equivalent products. To help confirm a **Pattaya golf price match guarantee** request, make sure the published material shows both the terms and the price. If the details are unclear, the administrator may ask for the information needed to compare them.

Prepare your request in this order:

1. Choose the TRounding product you want to book.
2. Check that the other provider's published offer has the same terms.
3. Prepare a view showing the product terms and price together.
4. Send it to the administrator through Naver Band chat.

## 5% rewards and no card surcharge

Every TRounding product earns **5% of the payment amount in rewards**, with no expiry date. For example, a THB 2,750 product earns approximately THB 137. Ask through Naver Band chat about how to use your rewards.

Cash and card prices are the same, with no additional card surcharge. Once your **Pattaya golf price match guarantee** request is confirmed, you can choose your payment method without a price difference for paying by card.

## Golf products and transfers are booked separately

The price match guarantee applies to products sold by TRounding. TRider transfers are not covered and have separate fixed fares. Golf transfers are priced per vehicle and include a return journey between Pattaya and the golf course, with the driver waiting during the round. Check vehicle-specific fares at [TRider](https://trider.taxi/).

If you also need an airport or golf course transfer, arrange your **Pattaya golf price match guarantee** request and your TRider booking separately.

Leaving Pattaya for your flight home? Start by checking which Bangkok airport is printed on your ticket. Suvarnabhumi (BKK) and Don Mueang (DMK) are different destinations, and your pickup arrangements should match the airport and departure date of your own flight.

This guide explains how to prepare a Pattaya-to-airport transfer with T-Rider without leaving important details until checkout.

## Choose BKK or DMK from your flight ticket

Do not choose the airport from the airline name alone. Check the airport code on the ticket, then select your Pattaya accommodation as the pickup location and the correct airport as your destination.

If you have a connecting itinerary, use the airport for the flight you will actually board in Thailand. Send the team any flight changes that affect your transfer.

## Work backwards from departure, not hotel checkout

Your hotel checkout time is not necessarily a suitable airport pickup time. Check your airline's check-in and bag-drop deadlines and allow for the road journey, possible traffic, finding your terminal and airport formalities.

Ask the team to help check a proposed pickup time against your hotel location and flight details. No road transfer can guarantee a fixed journey time in all traffic conditions. Keep a buffer rather than planning to arrive just before check-in closes.

For a flight just after midnight, check the calendar carefully: your hotel pickup may need to be on the previous date. Use the local Thailand date and time for the transfer and compare it with your flight ticket.

## Give the exact hotel entrance and luggage details

Provide the accommodation name and full location. A condominium, villa or hotel with several entrances may need a more precise meeting point than its main address. Confirm where you should wait and have your bags ready there at the agreed pickup time.

Count all passengers and all luggage, including any extra bags bought during your trip. Suitcases, golf bags and strollers take up different amounts of space. The booking form can recommend a vehicle from the passenger-and-luggage details you enter; check unusual or oversized items with the team before confirming.

## Review the quote for your particular trip

T-Rider quotes airport transfers per vehicle. The destination airport, vehicle and exact pickup address matter. Review the final booking quote rather than assuming an advertised base fare applies to every Pattaya address.

Highway tolls are included, and T-Rider states that there is no late-night surcharge. Address-based distance charges or requested extras may affect the total. Tell the team in advance if you need an extra stop or separate waiting time, and confirm the price and availability before relying on it.

Airport-arrival flight-delay waiting should not be interpreted as permission to postpone a hotel pickup automatically. If your departure flight or plans change, contact the team to confirm whether your booked pickup should change.

## Keep the booking and payment details handy

Check the airport, pickup date and time, hotel, vehicle and contact details in your booking confirmation. When vehicle information is provided, use it to identify the assigned vehicle rather than boarding a different car.

T-Rider does not require a deposit. Cash can be paid to the driver at your destination; card payment and bank transfer are arranged through the team. Confirm your preferred method before the trip.

For a trip that has not started, free cancellation through booking lookup is available when more than two hours remain before pickup. If you are closer to pickup, contact the team to check the applicable arrangements.

## Return-transfer checklist

- Correct airport code: BKK or DMK.
- Thailand pickup date and time, checked against the flight departure.
- Exact accommodation entrance and meeting instructions.
- All passengers, suitcases and oversized luggage included.
- Final per-vehicle quote and any requested extras checked.
- Booking number and a working contact method ready.

Checked 26 November 2026. All times are local to Thailand. This TRider and Buddy Golf guide helps you plan meals, golf and transport.

## Check the current rule, not an old afternoon timetable

[TAT's current visitor advisory](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/05/alcohol-sales-and-consumption-rules-updated-in-thailand-what-tourists-need-to-know/) gives general sales hours of **11:00–24:00**, effective 29 May 2026, including the former 14:00–17:00 gap. This does not mean every venue can sell around the clock. [The government summary](https://thailand.go.th/public/issue-focus-detail/alcohol-sales-and-consumption-rules-updated-in-thailand) also explains venue-specific conditions, public-place restrictions and the legal drinking age of 20.

[The November government notice](https://thailand.go.th/public/index.php/guide-book-detail/hotels-tourist-venues-exempt-from-thailands-buddhist-holiday-alcohol-ban) retains restrictions on five major Buddhist holidays, with defined venue exemptions. Check the actual date and venue eligibility; a Pattaya restaurant or golf course is not automatically exempt. Election restrictions require separate checks against the relevant official notice.

## Fit transport around the rest of your day

For airport transfers, send BKK or DMK, the flight, Thai arrival date, hotel address, passengers and luggage. Give return-flight and hotel-pickup details separately. If you plan to drink, arrange transport rather than driving yourself. Read our [airport booking guide](/blog/thailand-airport-taxi-booking-guide-en) and [book a vehicle](https://trider.taxi/booking?lang=en) after reviewing its final quote.

For Buddy Golf, send preferred dates, actual players, course and hotel, plus any airport or course transfers. International golf enquiries are for **private rounds with your own group of at least two actual players**. Course acceptance, tee time, price and inclusions need confirmation. Transport-only bookings have no two-golfer requirement, and a vehicle form does not confirm golf. [Ask about the whole itinerary on WhatsApp](https://wa.me/66815693445).

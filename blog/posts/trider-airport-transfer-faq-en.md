Here are clear answers to ten common questions about booking a Bangkok airport transfer. TRider operates between Suvarnabhumi (BKK) and Don Mueang (DMK) airports, Bangkok and Pattaya.

## 1. Which payment methods can I use?

You can pay by cash, card or bank transfer.

## 2. Are there restrictions on cancellations or refunds?

There are no restrictions on cancellations or refunds.

## 3. Are tolls extra from BKK to Pattaya?

Tolls are included in TRider's fixed fare from BKK to Pattaya.

| Vehicle | Fixed fare from BKK to Pattaya |
| --- | ---: |
| Sedan | THB 1,000 |
| SUV | THB 1,300 |
| Van | THB 1,700 |

## 4. Is there a late-night surcharge?

There is no late-night surcharge. Online booking is available 24 hours a day.

## 5. Will the driver wait if my flight is delayed?

The driver waits free of charge when your flight is delayed, with no waiting-time limit.

## 6. Which vehicle should I choose?

| Vehicle | Passengers | Suitcases | Golf bags |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Sedan | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| SUV | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| Van | 6 | 6 | 6 |

Check both your group size and the number of suitcases or golf bags before choosing.

## 7. Where is the meeting point at Suvarnabhumi Airport?

Meet the driver at **Gate 7**. Detailed meeting instructions are available after booking.

## 8. Can I request a name board?

A name board is available as a paid optional service.

## 9. Can I check the assigned vehicle in advance?

You can see a photo of the assigned vehicle in advance. The driver also uses a dedicated app for trip operations.

## 10. When can I book?

Online booking is available 24 hours a day. The booking screen supports Korean, English, Chinese, Japanese and Thai.

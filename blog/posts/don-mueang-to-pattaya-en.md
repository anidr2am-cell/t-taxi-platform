Arriving at Don Mueang and heading straight to Pattaya? A private airport transfer lets you arrange the journey to your accommodation before you land. The important details are not just the fare: your arrival airport, hotel address, luggage and pickup instructions all help your trip run smoothly.

This guide explains what to check when booking a Don Mueang Airport to Pattaya transfer with T-Rider.

## Start with the correct airport and destination

Select Don Mueang (DMK) in your booking, rather than Suvarnabhumi (BKK). They are separate airport pickup locations, so an airport mix-up can affect your arrangements.

Enter your flight details and the full address of your Pattaya accommodation. A hotel name alone may be ambiguous. If you are staying at a condominium or private accommodation, include the building name and the correct entrance or reception location where known. Check the destination before confirming the booking.

## Compare the final quote, not just the headline fare

T-Rider’s advertised airport pickup base fares are per vehicle. Highway tolls, airport parking and flight-delay waiting are included.

Your final quote may also include an address-based distance charge or an optional name-sign fee. Review the fare for your exact pickup, destination and vehicle rather than treating a base fare as a universal total. If something is unclear, ask the team before booking.

## Choose a vehicle for people and luggage

Add every passenger, including children and infants, and enter the number and size of your bags. Golf bags and oversized items also need to be included when choosing transport.

T-Rider’s booking form recommends a vehicle for the passenger-and-luggage combination you enter. If you are carrying unusually large items or need a child seat, check with the team before confirming. Do not assume a smaller car will fit simply because it has enough seats.

## Keep your flight and contact details accurate

T-Rider states that airport pickup timing adjusts to your actual arrival if your flight is delayed. Flight-delay waiting is included. Enter your flight details accurately and contact the team if your itinerary changes.

After booking, send your booking number through an official contact channel shown on the confirmation screen to arrange trip updates and receive vehicle and driver information.

## Use your confirmed Don Mueang pickup instructions

Follow the meeting instructions provided for your own booking. Do not use another airport’s meeting-point guide as a substitute for your Don Mueang instructions. If you cannot find the meeting location or assigned vehicle, contact the team before leaving the arranged area.

## Payment and changes

T-Rider does not require a deposit. You can pay cash to the driver at your destination; card payments are arranged through the team. Confirm your preferred payment method before travel.

For a trip that has not started, cancellation is free when more than two hours remain before pickup. If pickup is closer, contact the team to check the applicable arrangements.

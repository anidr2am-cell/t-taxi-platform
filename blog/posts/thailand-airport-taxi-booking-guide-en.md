Booking an airport transfer in Thailand is easier when you gather the right details before opening the form. An accurate airport, flight, destination and luggage list help the team arrange a vehicle for your actual trip.

Use this checklist for T-Rider pickups between Bangkok airports and accommodation in Bangkok or Pattaya.

## 1. Identify your arrival airport

Check the airport code on your ticket, not just the city name:

- **BKK:** Suvarnabhumi Airport. See [BKK to Pattaya](/blog/suvarnabhumi-to-pattaya-en) or [BKK to Bangkok](/blog/suvarnabhumi-to-bangkok-city-en).
- **DMK:** Don Mueang Airport. See [DMK to Pattaya](/blog/don-mueang-to-pattaya-en).

These are different airports. Do not copy the route, meeting point or price from one airport into a booking for the other.

## 2. Check the arriving flight and Thailand date

For a connecting journey, enter the flight segment that actually arrives in Thailand. Compare the flight number with your ticket and confirm the local arrival date and time.

Overnight flights and time-zone differences can make the departure date in your home country different from the arrival date in Thailand. Read the booking screen's date guidance and check any automatically displayed pickup details before confirming. Your flight's departure time is not its arrival time.

If the airline changes your itinerary later, tell the team and check that your transfer details have been updated. Flight-delay pickup timing is adjusted to the actual arrival, but a changed flight number or date still needs attention.

## 3. Confirm the accommodation location

Use the accommodation's full name and map location, then compare them with your hotel confirmation. If a hotel has several branches, identify the correct branch. For a condominium or villa, add the building and entrance details if known.

Tell the team about an extra stop or a route that is not a direct airport-to-accommodation transfer. Confirm availability and the quote rather than assuming an additional stop is included.

## 4. Count people and luggage separately

Include every passenger, including children and infants. Then count suitcases, golf bags, strollers and other bulky items. If a bag is unusually large, describe its size.

T-Rider's booking form recommends a vehicle based on the passenger-and-luggage combination entered. Do not add different maximum luggage allowances together and assume they all fit at once. Ask the team about a child seat or unusual equipment before booking.

## 5. Review the final quote and options

Airport pickup fares are per vehicle. Tolls, airport parking and flight-delay waiting are included, and T-Rider states that there is no late-night surcharge. An exact address or selected option can still affect the total: check address-based distance charges and any paid name-sign option on your own booking.

Read the [airport taxi fare checklist](/blog/thailand-airport-taxi-fare-tips-en) if you are comparing quotes. Confirm your payment method and change conditions before you travel.

## 6. Save the confirmation and arrange trip updates

After booking, check the airport, flight, pickup date and time, destination, passenger count and vehicle. Keep your booking number handy and use an official contact channel shown on the confirmation screen for trip updates and vehicle information.

Follow the meeting instructions issued for your own airport and booking. A name board is a separately requested paid service. The [Suvarnabhumi pickup guide](/blog/suvarnabhumi-airport-pickup-guide-en) is for BKK arrivals, not a substitute for DMK instructions.

## 7. Check the return journey separately

An airport pickup does not automatically book your return. If you also need a hotel-to-airport transfer, check the departing airport and choose a pickup time with enough margin for travel and airline check-in. See [Pattaya to Bangkok airports](/blog/pattaya-to-airport-transfer-en).

## Before you confirm

You should be able to answer five questions: Which airport? Which arriving flight and Thailand date? Which accommodation entrance? How many people and bags? What is the final quote?

Planning solo golf in Pattaya? Buddy Golf offers group joining 365 days a year, with a group to play with even when you travel alone. Send your preferred dates, courses and accommodation through [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/66815693445) to discuss golf, joining a round and TRider vehicle transfers together.

## Join a Pattaya golf group 365 days a year

You do not need to find your own playing partners before travelling. Tell us when you would like to play, and we will discuss the actual course, tee time, price and meeting arrangements with you. Year-round joining does not guarantee a particular course or tee time, or partners of a particular nationality, language or playing level.

Through [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/66815693445), share your golf dates and transport plans, and ask about the group and meeting arrangements. Copy the short enquiry below and fill in what you know; write “not decided” where needed.

## Keep golf fees and shared vehicle costs clear

Confirm whether green fees, caddie fees, cart fees and caddie tips are included in the golf quote. Travellers sharing a vehicle can divide the vehicle's transfer charge among the actual passengers. That does not mean golf playing fees are shared in the same way or included in the vehicle price.

Use a quote for your intended date and course, and check the inclusions before confirming the total budget.

## Share your accommodation and luggage details

Players on the same golf date may stay at different hotels or need different pickup points. Give the actual pickup address, total passenger count and the number and size of golf bags and suitcases.

If the trip involves several accommodation stops or additional waiting, explain the full itinerary and confirm the conditions. Vehicle suitability depends on the actual combination of passengers and luggage.

## Arrange round-trip golf transport

TRider provides round-trip transfers between Pattaya and golf courses. The [Pattaya golf transfer price guide](/blog/pattaya-golf-transfer-price-en) explains course groups, vehicles, holes and driver waiting during the round.

Once the playing arrangements are confirmed, check the pickup location and schedule together. If you want to share a vehicle, the team can check whether it suits the full group's luggage as well as the passengers.

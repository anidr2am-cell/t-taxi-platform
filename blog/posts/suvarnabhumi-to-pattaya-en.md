Travelling from Suvarnabhumi Airport to Pattaya? You can take an airport taxi, a bus or a pre-booked private transfer. TRider's BKK-to-Pattaya base fares are **THB 1,000 for a sedan, THB 1,300 for an SUV and THB 1,700 for a van, per vehicle**. Choose by passenger count, luggage and arrival plans, then check the final price for your hotel address.

Ready to arrange pickup? [Open the airport pickup booking form in English](/booking?service=AIRPORT_PICKUP&lang=en). Select **Suvarnabhumi (BKK)** and enter your Pattaya destination; Don Mueang (DMK) is a different airport and route.

## Quick comparison

| Option | What to expect | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Airport taxi | Arrange a taxi after landing | Travellers who want to find a car on arrival |
| Bus | Uses fixed boarding points and schedules | Travellers with light luggage and a flexible schedule |
| TRider fixed-price transfer | Check the assigned car and driver in advance, then meet at Gate 7 | Travellers who want the price and vehicle confirmed before departure |

Airport taxi and bus conditions can change, so check the latest information before you travel.

## TRider fares from BKK to Pattaya

The airport route's base fare includes highway tolls and airport parking. There is no late-night surcharge. Check the final booking amount for any address-based distance charge or optional service you select.

| Vehicle | Base fare per vehicle |
| --- | ---: |
| Sedan | THB 1,000 |
| SUV | THB 1,300 |
| Van | THB 1,700 |

These are vehicle prices, not prices per passenger. For example, two people sharing the THB 1,000 sedan base fare pay **THB 500 each** before any address-based charge or selected extras.

[Check your BKK-to-Pattaya pickup price](/booking?service=AIRPORT_PICKUP&lang=en) using your actual hotel address and luggage details before confirming.

## Choose a vehicle for passengers and luggage

Enter the number of passengers, suitcases and golf bags together. A vehicle should fit your luggage as well as your group; do not choose by passenger count alone. If you are unsure about an unusual luggage combination, [ask on WhatsApp](https://wa.me/66815693445) before booking.

## What happens if my flight is delayed?

If your flight is delayed, pickup is adjusted to your actual arrival time. You can also check a photo of the assigned vehicle before the trip.

Enter the correct flight details when booking and use the contact details in your booking instructions if your plans change.

## Where do I meet the driver?

The TRider meeting point at Suvarnabhumi Airport is **Gate 7**. Keep your booking details and assigned vehicle photo ready, and follow the meeting instructions supplied for your reservation. A name board is a paid optional service, rather than an automatically included extra.

The public taxi queue and bus boarding points use their own procedures. For a reserved TRider pickup, follow [the BKK driver meeting guide](/blog/suvarnabhumi-airport-pickup-guide-en). Do not apply BKK Gate 7 instructions to DMK.

## Payment and cancellation

No deposit is required. Pay cash to the driver after arriving at your destination, or contact the administrator for bank transfer and card payment. You can cancel through booking lookup free of charge and receive a full refund before the trip starts, provided more than 2 hours remain before pickup.

## Book your Suvarnabhumi Airport to Pattaya transfer

Have your arrival date, flight details, Pattaya hotel address, passenger count and luggage details ready. Open [airport pickup booking in English](/booking?service=AIRPORT_PICKUP&lang=en), choose BKK and your destination, review the vehicle and final price, then confirm your reservation. Save the booking details so you can find the meeting instructions after landing.

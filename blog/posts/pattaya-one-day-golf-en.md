If your group wants one round during a Pattaya holiday, choose a day between hotel check-in and the trip back to the airport. Buddy Golf takes enquiries from international visitors for private rounds with at least two people actually playing.

## Leave two mornings available

On a three-night stay, you could set aside the second morning as your first choice and the third as a backup. Wait for the course and tee time to be confirmed before attaching a sightseeing or dinner reservation to that day. If you have a preferred course, tell us whether the course or the time matters more to your group.

## Tell us how many are playing and how many are riding

Send your dates, player count, hotel and golf bag count. If the people riding in the vehicle differ from the people playing, give both counts. Buddy Golf and TRider can discuss the round and hotel-to-course transport together. Check the golf quote per player and the transport quote per vehicle. The course, time and price depend on what can be confirmed for your dates.

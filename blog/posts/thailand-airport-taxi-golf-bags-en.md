Two passengers with two golf travel cases and two large suitcases need a different luggage check from two passengers with cabin bags. For a Bangkok or Pattaya hotel transfer, list everything the vehicle must carry before choosing a vehicle. [Check your TRider airport pickup options](/booking?service=AIRPORT_PICKUP&lang=en).

## Give the size of the longest bag

Count suitcases, golf bags and cabin bags separately. Mention rigid cases and give the length of the longest item. For bulky luggage, share its three dimensions or a photo in a consultation. Fit depends on the actual vehicle, seats in use and your luggage combination.

## Check the whole vehicle quote

Use the arrival airport on your ticket and the full hotel address. Compare the suggested vehicle and final quote before booking. TRider quotes vehicle fares per vehicle, and the vehicle fare is the same for card and cash. For the trip back to the airport, use the [airport drop-off form](/booking?service=AIRPORT_DROPOFF&lang=en). [Ask about your luggage on WhatsApp](https://wa.me/66815693445).

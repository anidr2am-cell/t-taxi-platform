Pattaya golf transfer prices depend on the course group, number of holes and vehicle type. The base fares below are **per vehicle**, including **round-trip travel between Pattaya and the golf course, plus driver waiting during the round**. These transfer fares do not vary by season or day of the week.

## Pattaya golf transfer price table

All amounts are in Thai baht (THB). These are vehicle transfer charges, rather than golf playing fees.

## Calculate the transfer cost per person

The Group A and B van base fare for an 18-hole round is THB 2,300 per vehicle. If six travellers share that vehicle, the calculation is `2,300 ÷ 6`, or **about THB 383 each for transport only**.

This is a cost-sharing example, not a promise about vehicle capacity or luggage space. Confirm that the vehicle suits the group's actual luggage. Green fees, caddie fees, cart fees and tips are separate golf costs; they are not divided or included simply because travellers share a transfer.

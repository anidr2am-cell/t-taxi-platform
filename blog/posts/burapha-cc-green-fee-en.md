For a clearer Pattaya golf budget, look at the **Burapha Golf Club green fee** and your transfer cost separately. The green fees below are per person and include the caddie fee and cart fee; caddie tips are separate. Transfers are priced per vehicle, so only each passenger's share of the transport cost changes with the number travelling.

## Burapha Golf Club green fees by season

Burapha's high season runs from **October 16 to March 31**. The general high season is November 1 to March 31, but some golf courses apply high-season rates from October.

| Season | Weekday per person | Weekend per person | Included |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| High season | THB 2,750 | THB 3,150 | Caddie fee and cart fee |
| Low season | THB 2,350 | THB 2,650 | Caddie fee and cart fee |

These **Burapha Golf Club green fees** do not include caddie tips. Before booking, check the season and whether your chosen date falls on a weekday or weekend.

## Return transfers to Burapha Golf Club

Burapha is in Group B of TRider's golf transfer fare groups. These fixed fares are per vehicle and include the return trip between Pattaya and the course, plus driver waiting during the round. They do not change by season or day of the week.

| Vehicle | 18 holes | 27 holes | 36 holes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Sedan | THB 1,800 | THB 2,100 | THB 2,400 |
| SUV | THB 2,000 | THB 2,400 | THB 2,600 |
| Van | THB 2,300 | THB 2,600 | THB 2,900 |

The price match guarantee applies to products sold by TRounding. TRider transfers are not covered and have separate fixed fares.

## Per-person cost for 18 holes on a high-season weekday

Add each passenger's share of the 18-hole return transfer to the high-season weekday **Burapha Golf Club green fee** of THB 2,750:

| Party and vehicle | Green fee | Transfer share per person | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2 people · sedan | THB 2,750 | THB 900 | THB 3,650 |
| 3 people · SUV | THB 2,750 | Approx. THB 667 | Approx. THB 3,417 |
| 4 people · van | THB 2,750 | THB 575 | THB 3,325 |
| 6 people · van | THB 2,750 | Approx. THB 383 | Approx. THB 3,133 |

Green fees, caddie fees and cart fees stay the same per person regardless of party size. Only the transfer cost is shared between passengers. Travelling together does not reduce the **Burapha Golf Club green fee** itself.

## Choose a vehicle for your passengers and golf bags

A sedan carries up to 2 passengers and 2 golf bags, an SUV up to 3 passengers and 3 golf bags, and a van up to 6 passengers and 6 golf bags. Check both the number of travellers and the number of bags before choosing.

When combining the **Burapha Golf Club green fee** with transport, stay within the vehicle's capacity. For 4 or 6 passengers, divide the THB 2,300 van fare for 18 holes by the actual number travelling.

Green fees, caddie fees and cart fees stay the same per person regardless of party size. Only the transfer cost is shared between passengers.
